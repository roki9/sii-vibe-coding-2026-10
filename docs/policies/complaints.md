# Example Complaint Procedure — Poland

Version: CMP-PL-0.1. Reference review: 2026-10-18. Status: sample operating procedure, not company-approved terms.

## Applicability

CMP-01: Use for Polish consumer complaints to the seller concerning non-conformity of electronics under contracts concluded from 2023. Manufacturer guarantees, older contracts and uncertain buyer status require employee routing to the applicable rules.

## Sourced consumer baseline

CMP-02: Seller liability generally covers non-conformity revealed within two years of delivery. Purchase date alone is insufficient to decide the delivery-based period. Do not automatically reject an older case; timing and applicable rights need review.

CMP-03: Repair and replacement are principal remedies; price reduction or withdrawal may apply under the statutory conditions. Obtain the customer's requested remedy and relevant repair history rather than promising an immediate refund.

CMP-04: A receipt is not the only permitted proof of purchase. Do not refuse intake because the receipt is missing.

CMP-05: A complaint response is generally required within 14 days of receipt; failure has statutory consequences. AI receipt/acknowledgment is not the company's substantive response. Escalation or AI failure does not restart the period.

Source for CMP-02–05: [UOKiK — Non-conformity](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/reklamacja/niezgodnosc/), referring to the Consumer Rights Act, including Articles 7a and 43a–43e. This is a focused summary, not exhaustive legal coverage.

## Example company workflow

CMP-06: Save receipt of submission independently of AI processing. Show a reference and received date. Queue unresolved cases for employees with the received timestamp visible.

CMP-07: Record customer-reported symptoms, delivery date, onset date, requested remedy and relevant previous attempts. Ask for missing facts in chat without preventing saved intake.

CMP-08: Assess visible damage separately from reported functionality. Possible impact or liquid exposure is a hypothesis until supported by additional evidence. A photo does not establish customer fault.

CMP-09: Recommend inspection where causation, defect origin or remedy applicability remains uncertain. Do not reject a complaint merely because no damage is visible or packaging is absent.

CMP-10: Only an employee confirms the recorded outcome. A negative outcome requires supporting facts, applicable grounds and a public explanation; an override preserves the AI recommendation.

CMP-11: Do not ask customers to dismantle equipment, charge a swollen battery or energize visibly unsafe devices. Escalate handling instructions to employees.

CMP-12: The portal's required image is a workflow constraint, not a condition of statutory rights. Display a company-approved alternative complaint channel before production.

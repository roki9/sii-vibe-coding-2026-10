# Example Return Procedure — Poland

Version: RET-PL-0.1. Reference review: 2026-10-18. Status: sourced consumer baseline plus sample workflow; no actual voluntary company return terms supplied.

## Statutory withdrawal route

RET-01: Determine purchase channel before assessing eligibility. For ordinary online consumer purchases, the usual withdrawal period is 14 days from receipt of the goods; purchase date is not a substitute. Ask for receipt date and declaration date/intention. Missing information about withdrawal rights, exceptional sales contexts and disputed dates require employee review rather than automatic refusal.

Source: [UOKiK — Withdrawal time limits](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/terminy-odstapienie/), Consumer Rights Act Articles 27–29.

RET-02: Separate withdrawal eligibility from condition and value. Use beyond what is needed to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods may involve diminished-value responsibility; visible use alone does not cancel withdrawal. Never calculate a deduction from one photograph.

Source: [UOKiK — Withdrawal questions and answers](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/pytania-i-odpowiedzi/odstapienie-od-umowy/).

RET-03: Potential statutory exceptions require relevant facts and employee verification. Examples include individualized goods, certain opened sealed hygiene goods and opened sealed software media. Do not assume all opened electronics or all headphones are excluded.

Source: [UOKiK — Exceptions](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/wylaczenia-prawa-do-odstapienia/), Article 38.

RET-04: Withdrawal and physically returning goods have separate timelines. Generally, goods should be sent back within 14 days of withdrawal; the seller generally refunds within 14 days of receiving the declaration, with statutory withholding conditions. Do not treat these as the same deadline or promise that this application transfers money.

Source: [UOKiK — Effects of withdrawal](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/prawo-odstapienia-od-umowy/skutek/), Articles 32 and 34.

## Voluntary return route

RET-05: A change-of-mind return for an in-store purchase must be assessed against an actual adopted seller policy. No such policy has been supplied: route to employee review. Do not invent a 14-day or 30-day store-return promise.

RET-06: Illustrative optional company provision, inactive until adopted: the seller may offer a defined additional return window with condition and completeness requirements. An adopted provision must specify its dates, eligible products and remedy, and must not restrict statutory rights. This placeholder is not a rule granting or refusing a return.

## Example company workflow

RET-07: Report observable condition separately from legal eligibility. Mention scratches, visible wear and accessories only when actually visible; hidden sides and functionality remain unverified.

RET-08: “No visible wear” means only that the supplied photo shows none. Resale as new requires physical inspection, functionality, completeness and appropriate device-data handling outside this MVP.

RET-09: Ask whether the customer wants advice or intends to declare withdrawal. Record an explicit declaration and its timestamp separately from preliminary advice. Never tell the customer that an AI failure voided an already saved declaration.

RET-10: Employee confirmation is required for the operational outcome. Potential deductions and policy exceptions require documented employee review. Preserve recommendation history and explain next steps without executing payment or logistics.

RET-11: Required photo submission does not limit other lawful withdrawal channels. Provide the company's alternative contact route.
